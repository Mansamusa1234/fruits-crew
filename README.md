# Fruits Crew

Created by Darren-neil.

**The whole living world is one family.**  
Everything is connected. Each one teach one.  
Learn together. Grow together. Love one another.

Fruits Crew is a joyful worldwide children’s learning and animation universe where fruits, vegetables, seeds, soil, roots, water, weather, plants, animals and the Earth can talk, sing and learn together.

Child-facing: Come meet the world that talks!  
Parent-facing: Original songs, stories and adventures that turn screen time into family learning.

Caribbean culture is one part of a global world, alongside African, British, Asian, European, Middle Eastern, Pacific, Indigenous and other cultures.

Original characters and lyrics. Not a copy of any other children’s brand.

## Working product

- Home (worldwide living universe)

- Plant explorer and world map

## Safety

- No child-to-child chat
- No ads
- No products sold to children here
- Wild plants: never eat unless a knowledgeable adult has identified them
- Pretend money only
