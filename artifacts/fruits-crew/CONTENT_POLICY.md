# Content Policy

## Educational Principle

Never present an unverified claim as established scientific fact.

Use clear categories:

- Scientifically established
- Historical record
- Traditional use
- Oral tradition
- Folklore
- Hypothesis
- Mystery
- Still being investigated

Characters model: “Let’s investigate!”

## Sources

Prioritise:

- Universities
- Museums
- Botanical gardens
- Peer-reviewed research
- Government scientific bodies
- Recognised historical archives

Traditional knowledge is welcome and respected — and labelled appropriately.

## Wild Plants

Every wild-plant lesson must include:

> Never eat a wild plant unless a knowledgeable adult has positively identified it as safe.

## Medical Claims

Do not make medical-treatment claims about food or plants. Traditional use is historical/cultural context, not clinical advice.

## Spiritual Framing

A Creation-centred worldview is allowed. Never attack a child’s religion or lack of religion. Distinguish spiritual framing from scientific explanation where appropriate.

## Originality

Do not imitate protected characters, songs, logos or visual styles of other programmes. Build original IP.
