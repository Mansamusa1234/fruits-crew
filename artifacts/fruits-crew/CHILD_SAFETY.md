# Child Safety

Fruits Crew is designed for children and must comply with relevant law and platform policy before public launch.

## Design Goals

- Minimal personal data collection
- Parental controls and age-band selection
- No behavioural advertising directed at children
- No unrestricted child-to-stranger messaging
- No public child profiles
- No precise child location collection unless legally justified and necessary
- Clear separation of adult community features from child experience

## Research Before Launch

- UK GDPR & Age Appropriate Design Code (Children’s Code)
- COPPA (US)
- GDPR (EU)
- Apple App Store & Google Play child policies
- Any other jurisdiction of launch

## Content Safeguards

- Wild plant content always includes: never eat a wild plant unless a knowledgeable adult has positively identified it as safe.
- AI Nature Guide (Phase 6) will have strict age-appropriate filters, refuse dangerous instructions, and encourage adult supervision for real-world experiments.
- Evidence vs traditional claim labelling is part of the educational model.

## Reporting

Moderation and reporting tools will be built for adult community and content systems.
