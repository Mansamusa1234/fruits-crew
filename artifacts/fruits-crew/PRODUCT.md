# Product

## Core Message

**Everything Is Connected. Each One Teach One.**

## Primary Audience

Children aged 2–12, guided by parents and teachers.

## Platforms

- Web (mobile-first, PWA)
- iOS & Android (Phase 8)
- Tablets
- Desktop
- Smart TVs (future)

## MVP Scope

- Homepage
- 8 core characters
- ~50 plant entries
- 3 educational stories
- 3 original songs (placeholders initially)
- Maths game prototype
- Plant explorer
- Virtual garden prototype
- Parent account + child profiles
- Progress tracking
- Basic admin dashboard

## Character Team (Core 8)

1. **Soursop** — Wise investigator
2. **Banana** — Comic heart, counting & movement
3. **Mango** — Explorer, geography & cultures
4. **Coconut** — Inventor, engineering
5. **Pineapple** — Creative performer, music
6. **Fig** — Keeper of stories, history
7. **Dandelion** — Resilient wild plant
8. **Little Seed** — The child’s proxy; asks “Why?”

## Kingdoms (expanding)

- Fruits Crew
- Wild Plant Crew
- Root Crew
- Seed & Grain Crew
- Tree Council
- Flower Kingdom
- Pollinator Crew
- Soil Squad
- Animal Kingdom
- Ocean Crew
- Elemental Guides (Sunny, Aqua, Breezy, Terra, Luna)

## Educational Principle

Never present unverified claims as scientific fact.  
Use clear categories: scientifically established, historical record, traditional use, folklore, hypothesis, mystery.

## Recurring Phrases

- Back to Nature
- Everything Is Connected

- Grow Your Mind
- Ask. Investigate. Discover.
- From Seed to Knowledge
