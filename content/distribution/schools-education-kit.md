# Schools and libraries — Fruits Crew

Use with the `/schools` page.

**Wake Up, Little Seed** (15–20 min, Nursery–Year 2)

1. Listen once.
2. Children curl, then grow on the chorus.
3. Ask what a seed needs: water, warmth, time.
4. Home: jar beans with an adult. Do not eat unknown seeds.

**Count the Mangoes** (10 min)

Clap 1–10. Count real fruit. Do not rush.

**Roots Hold Hands** (15 min)

Link fingers back-to-back. Name hidden helpers. Do not pull plants up.

Adults stay in the room. No child-to-child messaging on our site.
