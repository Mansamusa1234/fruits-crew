# Press kit — Fruits Crew

**Name:** Fruits Crew  
**Creator:** Darren-neil  
**Tagline:** The whole living world is one family.  
**Child:** Come meet the world that talks!  
**Parent:** Original songs, stories and adventures that turn screen time into family learning.

## Boilerplate

Fruits Crew is a joyful worldwide children’s learning and animation universe where fruits, vegetables, seeds, soil, roots, water, weather, plants, animals and the Earth can talk, sing and learn together. Caribbean culture is one part of a global family, alongside African, British, Asian, European, Middle Eastern, Pacific, Indigenous and other living cultures.

## Do not say

- That Fruits Crew is only a Caribbean fruit crew
- That it is a copy of any other children’s franchise
- That children should eat a wild plant from a picture
